# Mbia MVP — Visual Design Guidelines

**Version:** 0.1

## 1. Direction

The MVP visual identity should be:

```text
clean
minimal
warm
human
soft and friendly
```

Avoid a cold corporate appearance and avoid making “African” identity a superficial collection of visual clichés.

The product's African relevance should first come from how the domain understands family structures and heritage. Cultural visual identity can remain subtle and intentional.

## 2. Layout

Prefer:

- generous whitespace;
- low information density;
- clear section hierarchy;
- lightweight cards;
- responsive mobile-first layouts.

## 3. Typography

Use one highly readable sans-serif family with a small hierarchy:

```text
Page / display title
Section title
Body
Caption
```

Avoid excessive text styles.

## 4. Color

Use:

- one primary brand color (brown);
- optionally one accent (soft green);
- neutral backgrounds and text colors.

Do not rely on stereotypes such as blue = male, pink = female as a primary comprehension mechanism.

## 5. Photography

Family photography should be visually important.

When no image exists, use a restrained avatar or initials.

## 6. Person cards

Keep cards compact:

- photo/avatar;
- name;
- birth/death year;
- subtle border;
- very light or no shadow;
- rounded corners.

The focused Person must be identifiable without aggressive highlighting.

## 7. Components

Keep the MVP component set intentionally small:

```text
Button
IconButton
TextField
Select
Avatar
PersonCard
MemoryCard
BottomSheet
Modal
Toast
NavigationBar
Skeleton
```

## 8. Animation

Allowed:

- smooth tree recentering;
- opening Person quick view;
- lightweight success feedback.

Avoid decorative, long or blocking animation.

## 9. Accessibility

Minimum expectations:

- sufficient contrast;
- readable type size;
- comfortable touch targets;
- keyboard support on desktop;
- form labels;
- state not communicated only by color.

## 10. Performance perception

Render primary structure quickly.

Use thumbnails before full images and local loading placeholders instead of blank screens.
